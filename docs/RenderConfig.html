RenderConfig

unifies parameters across the entire modular stack.

copy

instantiate

(Fn [(Ref RenderConfig a)] RenderConfig)

copies a RenderConfig.

delete

instantiate

(Fn [RenderConfig] ())

deletes a RenderConfig. Should usually not be called manually.

init

instantiate

(Fn [RaymarchConfig, VisibilityConfig] RenderConfig)

creates a RenderConfig.

march

instantiate

(Fn [(Ref RenderConfig a)] (Ref RaymarchConfig a))

gets the march property of a RenderConfig.

prn

instantiate

(Fn [(Ref RenderConfig a)] String)

converts a RenderConfig to a string.

set-march

instantiate

(Fn [RenderConfig, RaymarchConfig] RenderConfig)

set-march!

(Fn [(Ref RenderConfig a), RaymarchConfig] ())

instantiate
