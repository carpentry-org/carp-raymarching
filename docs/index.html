carp-raymarching

A signed-distance-field raymarcher for Carp.

(load "git@github.com:carpentry-org/carp-raymarching@master")

Distance fields

  • Sdf — primitives and the CSG operators that combine them
  • Svo — sparse voxel octree, with SvoNode

Marching

  • Ray — origin and direction
  • Raymarch — the march itself, returning a RaymarchHit, tuned by RaymarchConfig
  • Visibility — shadows and ambient occlusion, tuned by VisibilityConfig

Shading

  • Material — albedo, roughness, metallic, emission
  • Shading — blinn-phong and occlusion, lit by Light
  • Scene — world composition, with SceneObject and SceneHit
  • Renderer — the pipeline config (RenderConfig) and a shading pass over one hit

The examples/ directory has runnable CPU renderers that write PPM files, one of them parallel over carp-job-system.
