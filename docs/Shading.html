Shading

provides pure equations for surface lighting.

apply-fog

defn

(Fn [(Ref (Vector3 Double) a), (Ref (Vector3 Double) b), Double, Double] (Vector3 Double)) 

                        (apply-fog color fog-color dist density)

blends a color with a background/fog color based on distance.

apply-occlusion

defn

(Fn [(Ref (Vector3 a) b), a, a] (Vector3 a)) 

                        (apply-occlusion color shadow ao)

modulates a color by shadow and AO factors.

blinn-phong

defn

(Fn [(Ref (Vector3 Double) a), (Ref (Vector3 Double) b), (Ref (Vector3 Double) c), (Ref (Vector3 Double) d), Double, e] (Vector3 Double)) 

                        (blinn-phong normal light-dir view-dir albedo roughness metallic)

calculates diffuse and specular reflection.

lambert

defn

(Fn [(Ref (Vector3 Double) a), (Ref (Vector3 Double) b), (Ref (Vector3 Double) c)] (Vector3 Double)) 

                        (lambert normal light-dir albedo)

calculates basic diffuse reflection.
